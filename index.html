What types of dance classes do you offer at Dannie's Dance?

We offer a diverse range of dance classes—including creative movement, adapted ballet, hip-hop, and more—all designed specifically for individuals with disabilities. Each class is tailored to various abilities and interests, ensuring a joyful and enriching experience for every participant.

Who are the instructors at Dannie's Dance, and what experience do they have working with individuals with disabilities?

Our instructors are highly experienced, compassionate, and specially trained in adaptive dance and education. They bring years of expertise working with individuals with disabilities, ensuring each class is both engaging and supportive.

What age groups do you serve at Dannie's Dance?

Dannie’s Dance welcomes individuals of all ages—from young children to adults. We offer age-appropriate classes that cater to the unique needs of each group, creating a nurturing and inclusive environment for everyone.

Are your classes inclusive and adapted to meet individual needs?

Absolutely. Every class at Dannie’s Dance is designed with inclusivity at its core. Our programs are carefully adapted to address the unique needs of each participant, ensuring a safe, supportive, and empowering experience.

What are the class sizes at Dannie's Dance, and is there one-on-one support available?

We keep our class sizes small to ensure personalized attention for every participant. Additionally, we offer one-on-one coaching sessions for individuals who require extra support, providing tailored guidance to meet their unique needs.

What are the benefits of dance for individuals with disabilities?

Dance at Dannie’s Dance promotes physical, emotional, and cognitive development. Participants often experience improved coordination, enhanced self-expression, increased confidence, and valuable social interactions—all within a supportive and uplifting community.

What is the cost of classes, and are there any financial assistance options available?

Our pricing is transparent and designed to be affordable. While costs vary by program, we also offer financial assistance and flexible payment options to ensure that every interested individual can benefit from our inclusive dance classes.

What is the process for registering for a class at Dannie's Dance, and is there a trial class available?

Registration is simple and can be completed online via our website or by contacting our team directly. We also offer a free trial class so you can experience our program firsthand before making a commitment.

What is your policy on sensory sensitivities and other individual needs?

We take sensory sensitivities and individual needs very seriously. Our facilities and class formats are designed to minimize sensory overload, and we work closely with participants and their families to accommodate any specific requirements for a comfortable, enjoyable experience.

What should my child/loved one wear or bring to class?

We recommend wearing comfortable, flexible clothing and appropriate footwear that allows ease of movement. For sessions that might involve water or creative messes, we suggest bringing a change of clothes. Specific guidelines will be provided upon registration to ensure a smooth experience.
